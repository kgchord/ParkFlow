  required design tension 7.4 kN x 2.0 = 14.8 kN -> confirm from anchor ETA/datasheet.

=> 16 mm plate selected: FoS >= 2 with NO credit for gusset edge support (10/12 mm fail the FoS 2.0 target

4. DEMO BOOM BARRIER (1.2 m boom, Al 6063-T5 rect tube 60x30x1.5)
==============================================================================
Section A = 261 mm2, Iv = 123316 mm4, Wv = 4111 mm3, Ih = 41506 mm4, Wh = 2767 mm3
Boom mass = 0.846 kg tube + 0.15 kg fittings = 0.996 kg  (0.705 kg/m)
Load case: self weight + 50 N vertical tip load (boom horizontal)
  M_hub = 66.7 Nm ; sigma = 16.2 MPa ; FoS(6063-T5) = 6.8
  Tip deflection = 3.7 mm  (L/327)
  50 N horizontal tip push (vehicle nudge): M = 60 Nm, sigma_h = 21.7 MPa, FoS = 5.1
  Carrier clamp: 2x M6 through-bolts @60 mm -> couple force 1112 N; bearing on 2x1.5 mm walls = 62 MPa vs 6063-T5 bearing ~1.5*Rm=240 -> FoS 3.9 (use crush tube / sleeve)
Breakaway shear pin (single shear, e = 35 mm from hinge): break at 100 N tip -> F_pin = 3300 N
  6061-T6 pin tau_u ~ 0.6 Rm = 174 MPa -> d = 4.91 mm -> use d = 5.0 mm (breaks at 104 N tip; 50 N service = 48% of break)
Static gravity torque of boom about shaft = 6.88 Nm
Counterweight: target 85% balance at 200 mm tail -> 2.98 kg required; MS plate 100x60x10 = 0.471 kg -> 7 plates = 3.30 kg INSTALLED (balance 94%, still boom-heavy)
  Residual gravity closing torque with the motor unpowered = 0.41 Nm = 0.34 N at the boom tip (cannot crush; breakaway pin covers lateral hits)
Motion 0->90 deg in 2.5 s, accel 0.4 s: omega = 0.75 rad/s (7.1 rpm), alpha = 1.87 rad/s2
Inertia: boom 0.622 kg m2, counterweight 0.132 kg m2
Operating wind 15 m/s (p=135 Pa) on RAISED boom, Cf 2.0, hub moment by projected face:
  30 mm face (wind in the rotation plane): 5.83 Nm ; worst oblique 48.5 mm at 32 deg: 9.44 Nm ; old 60 mm face: 11.66 Nm
Required torque WITHOUT counterweight (worst oblique) = 17.77 Nm   [60 mm face: 20.00]
Required torque WITH counterweight    (worst oblique) = 11.55 Nm   [60 mm face: 13.78]
Production selection: rated >= max(2.0 x 11.6 (with CW), 1.5 x 17.8 (CW-missing fault)) = 26.7 Nm at output speed >= 7 rpm  -> keep the >= 30 Nm specification for a 15 m/s operating-wind rating
  (specify a 24 V DC self-locking worm gearmotor with a DATASHEET rated torque >= 30 Nm, 10-15 rpm output, >= 12 mm shaft;
   no such part is in the BOM yet - see docs/OPEN_DECISIONS.md.)

BOM motor check (Lucas TVS 24 V wiper motor, listed 18 Nm; rated vs stall, speed, duty and self-locking NOT stated by the vendor):
  Margin at 15 m/s: 18 / 11.6 = 1.56 (target >= 2.0) -> SHORT for a 15 m/s rating
  Still-air requirement (gravity residual + acceleration + friction) = 2.12 Nm -> margin 8.5x
  Highest operating wind for margin 2.0 on 18 Nm = 12.8 m/s (46 km/h); for margin 1.5 = 15.4 m/s (55 km/h)
  => DEMO rating chosen: operate the boom only in wind <= 12 m/s (43 km/h); above that the boom stays lowered/pinned. Production rating (15 m/s) needs the >= 30 Nm motor.
  Check: at 12 m/s required 8.16 Nm, 18 Nm gives margin 2.21
  Mechanical power at peak (15 m/s case) = 8.6 W ; with worm eff. ~40% -> electrical ~ 27 W
  Motor current is NOT known (no vendor figure). The 24 V bus is sized for a 5 A firmware limit (IS sense); stall current to be bench-measured.
  Self-locking is NOT assumed anywhere in this design: holding is by the lowered-boom rest/stop and a storm-lock pin (below).

RAISED-BOOM SURVIVAL CASE: Delhi design gust 47 m/s -> p = 1325 Pa (IS 875-3, Kd=Ka=Kc=1.0), Cf 2.0, arm Lb/2 = 0.60 m
  back-driving moment at the hub: 30 mm face 57 Nm ; worst oblique (48.5 mm) 93 Nm ; 60 mm bounding face 115 Nm
  design value used for checks = 93 Nm (oblique); the earlier '100-115 Nm' quote was the 60 mm bounding face (115 Nm)
  20 mm stub shaft (MS E250): tau = T/Wp = 59 MPa (E250 shear yield ~0.58x250 = 145 MPa) -> FoS 2.5
  6x6x25 key: shear 62 MPa, bearing 124 MPa (E250 yield 250) -> FoS 2.0 bearing
  lateral reaction on the two UCFL204 bearings from the raised boom: 95 N total (30 mm face) -> well below a UC204 static rating (datasheet value to be confirmed)
  The 18 Nm motor/gearbox CANNOT be assumed to hold 93 Nm (stall 18 Nm, back-drive behaviour unknown).
  => the survival load path is a STORM-LOCK PIN through the hub/boom carrier into the frame (boom lowered or raised), not the gearbox.
     Storm rule (no anemometer in the BOM): on an IMD thunderstorm / dust-storm warning the guard lowers the boom and drops the pin.
     Pin check (PROPOSED, not yet in the CAD): 10 mm MS pin in double shear carries 93 Nm at a 60 mm lever = 1544 N -> tau = 10 MPa (FoS 14.8 vs 145 MPa). FEA/bench to confirm.

CLOSED-BOOM GUST vs BREAKAWAY PIN: gust moment about the vertical hinge = 1325 Pa x 2.0 x 60 mm x 1.155 m x 0.578 m = 106 Nm
  5 mm 6061-T6 pin breaks at about 120 Nm (nominal tau_u 0.6 Rm; scatter of +/-10 % is normal) -> gust = 89 % of break
  Wind speed that loads the pin to 50 % of its break value (the service margin used for the 50 N push) = 35.3 m/s
  => in the 47 m/s design gust the pin is at about 90 % of break: a nuisance shear is possible (fail-safe: the boom swings free). Keep spare pins (BOM).

  (2 mm Al canopy, 25 mm ventilated air gap, cuts absorbed solar ~80%; not included above).
