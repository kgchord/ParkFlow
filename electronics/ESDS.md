## 3. Architecture rationale (decision, why, rejected alternative)

1. **RS485 (THVD1450) for the RFID link, not plain UART or CAN.** The reader is on a cable of several metres next to a 24 V motor and contactor; differential signalling with a 3.3 V transceiver (supply 3.3-5 V, TI page) and an SM712 TVS tolerates that, and the reader class (IDT 85) is listed with RS485. Rejected: UART at TTL levels (no common-mode immunity over the cable), CAN (reader does not offer it; no extra benefit for one master and one slave).
2. **ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8 for the Lane Controller, not STM32.** Native USB-Serial-JTAG (GPIO19/20), 3.0-3.6 V single supply, ESP-IDF, enough GPIO for 6 opto inputs, HUB12, WS2812, RS485 and H-bridge; the BOM part is already priced and the team knows ESP-IDF. Cost: octal PSRAM takes GPIO35-37, and the module is rated -40 to 65 deg C (Espressif datasheet). Rejected: STM32 (better hard real-time and temperature range, but no budgeted part, no PCB library effort in the 4-month plan).
3. **Raspberry Pi 5 + AI HAT+ (Hailo-8) as Edge Node, not Jetson Orin Nano Super.** The HAT+ is in the BOM at 26 TOPS and the Pi is the cheapest path to HailoRT; Jetson stays the documented alternative. Cost: AI HAT+ is 0-50 deg C ambient (Raspberry Pi product brief), which the sealed enclosure does not currently meet. Rejected: Jetson (higher cost and power, not in BOM).
4. **PoE IP camera over RTSP, not a CSI camera.** A 2 MP IR bullet camera is weather-rated (IP67 per BOM listing), runs on 100 m Ethernet, and needs no Pi CSI port or long ribbon; PoE removes a power run. Cost: PoE support of the CP Plus model is "to be confirmed" in the BOM. Rejected: CSI (ribbon length limit about a metre, no IR/weather housing).
5. **Hardware interlocks in front of the H-bridge.** E-stop opens the K1 (LC1D09BD) coil supply (no firmware involved) and ESTOP_OK also gates the driver enable (MOT_INH) through U9; CLOSE is gated by BEAM_CLEAR through U10 and the CLOSE input of the module (MOT_IN_A) passes U19. A firmware hang cannot override either. Rejected: software-only interlocks (the safety case then depends on the firmware).
6. **IBT-2 (BTS7960) module for the barrier motor, not discrete BTN8982TA half-bridges.** One design is used everywhere (schematic, BOM, PCB). The module is rated 5.5-27 V motor supply, which covers the 21.6-28.8 V bus; the BTN8982TA is only specified 8-18 V normal, so it was dropped. The board carries a 1x8 header J14 (RPWM, LPWM, two enable lines tied to MOT_INH, IS_A, IS_B, +5 V, GND); 74AHCT1G08 gates U9, U10, U19 sit in front of it; the 1 k resistors on each IS pin are on the module. Motor power and return go straight to the module screw terminals and do not pass over the Lane Controller PCB. Caveat: IS scale (about 0.1 V/A) is a to-be-bench-calibrated figure. Rejected: BTN8982TA on our PCB (outside its normal voltage range), SMD half-bridges on the board (more copper, no benefit for a 5 A limit).
7. **Separate 24 V and 12 V supplies, PoE on its own adapter.** Isolates the noisy motor bus from the RFID reader and camera. Rejected: single 24 V with DC-DC for 12 V (adds a converter in the sealed box).

## 4. Power budget

Method: `tools/power_model.py` holds every number below; `tools/gen_power_tree.py` draws `diagrams/power_tree.png` from it. All loads are **target, to be bench-verified** except where a vendor page gave the figure (Section 12).

### 4.1 24 V bus (Mean Well LRS-200-24: 24 V, 8.8 A, 211.2 W, hiccup overload; Probots listing, Bengaluru)

| Device | V | Typ A | Peak A | Typ W | Peak W | Margin / note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Barrier motor, Lucas TVS 24 V 18 N.m via K1 + IBT-2 | 24 | 2.00 | 5.00 | 48.0 | 120.0 | ASSUMED: vendor page gives no current; 5 A = firmware limit (set from the IS sense, D7) |
| Lane Controller 24 V logic (F1, TPS54560, 5 V rail incl. P10 and ring) | 24 | 0.29 | 0.83 | 7.1 | 20.0 | 72 % margin to F1 3 A |
| 24 V field (6 opto inputs, sensors, buzzer) via F2 | 24 | 0.10 | 0.30 | 2.4 | 7.2 | 60 % to F2 0.75 A hold |
| K1 coil (LC1D09BD) + E-stop loop | 24 | 0.20 | 0.30 | 4.8 | 7.2 | coil current is an ASSUMPTION (not read from a Schneider sheet) |
| Edge chain: XL4015, X1202, Pi 5 + AI HAT+ | 24 | 0.41 | 1.23 | 9.9 | 29.4 | peak = 20 W load + 5 W UPS charge at 85 % |
| **Bus total** | 24 | **3.01** | **7.66** | **72** | **184** | typ 34 % of 8.8 A; **peak 87 %** (margin 13 %) |

Result: with the motor limited to 5 A the coincident peak is 7.66 A, inside the 8.8 A rating. The hiccup threshold is taken as 110 % of 8.8 A = 9.68 A (the 110 % figure is from the LRS-150 sheet; assumed the same here). The largest motor current that keeps the bus within the rating is 6.14 A, within the 80 % derated rating 4.38 A (others' peak 2.66 A). The earlier design (LRS-150-24, 6.5 A, 6 A motor limit) peaked at 8.66 A, 133 % of the supply (previously quoted as 8.56 A before the K1 coil was updated); that conflict is closed by the larger supply and the 5 A limit. Still open: the stall current of the wiper motor is unknown, and the 5 A limit is a firmware trip only (OPEN_DECISIONS D7). T-04 measures it.

### 4.4 PoE (Netgear GS305P, own adapter)

Budget 63 W (4 ports, 802.3af/at; max consumption 66.78 W; Netgear page). Camera target <= 15.4 W (802.3af class limit; camera draw not stated in the BOM listing): margin 76 % of the 63 W budget. The switch is rated 0-40 deg C (Netgear page): see 11 #8.

### 4.5 Mains side

LRS-200-24 input power at full load 211.2/0.89 = 237 W (efficiency taken from the earlier LRS-150 sheet figure, assumed equal; the LRS-200 sheet was not opened); LRS-100-12 102/0.85 (efficiency assumed) + GS305P 66.78 W = 187 W. At an assumed power factor of 0.6 (pessimistic, supplies without PFC): 1.72 A and 1.35 A per 6 A MCB, i.e. 3.5x headroom or more. Inrush: the LRS-150 sheet listed cold start 60 A at 230 VAC (the LRS-200 value was not read; assume similar); a 6 A C-curve MCB is expected to trip magnetically somewhere above 5-10 x In (IEC 60898 characteristic, not read on the A9N1P06C page), so one MCB per supply (as the BOM has) is the right grouping; confirm in T-01.

### 4.6 UPS ride-through arithmetic

Edge Node (Geekworm X1202, 2x 18650 per the Probots page): 2 cells x 2.5 Ah x 3.6 V = 18.0 Wh nominal (2.5 Ah is the low end of the BOM's 2500-3000 mAh). Usable = 18.0 x 0.80 (depth of discharge) x 0.85 (boost efficiency) x 0.80 (end-of-life capacity) = **9.8 Wh**. Required for 10 min at the 20 W peak: 20 x 10/60 = **3.3 Wh**. Margin **2.9x** (typical 8 W load: 7x). All derating factors are targets (T-10). Note: BOM_notes says the Geekworm board is described as 4-cell elsewhere; the budget uses 2 cells, the lower-capacity case.
RFID: 12 V mini UPS 27.5 Wh (BOM) x 0.7 = 19.3 Wh usable vs 1.5 A x 12 V x 10/60 = 3.0 Wh: **6.4x** (assumed 0.7 usable factor).
Lane Controller and barrier: **no ride-through exists** in the baseline. The supply holds up for tens of ms only (40 ms was the LRS-150 figure); the TPS3823-33 resets the ESP32 below 2.86-3.00 V (TI). Decision (SAFETY F11): the barrier **fails in place** on mains loss and is released by hand with a pin; the earlier claim of automatic fail-open is withdrawn. An optional 24 V battery buffer is OPEN_DECISIONS D2 (not in the BOM; the firmware AC_OK open-hold applies only if one is fitted). The PoE switch and camera have no UPS (D3), so ANPR stops in an outage while the Pi (X1202) and RFID (12 V mini UPS) ride through.

**E-stop path independent of firmware.** J4: +24V_FIELD -> E-stop NC contacts (external) -> K1 (LC1D09BD) coil -> Q4 (low side, driven by K1_EN). Opening the E-stop interrupts coil current by itself; firmware cannot close it. A second channel: ESTOP_OK (opto U17) feeds the U9 AND gate that drives MOT_INH (both enable pins of the IBT-2), so the bridge is disabled even if K1 contacts weld. The E-stop contacts are rated 1 A DC at 36 V (ProMax page) which is adequate for the contactor coil loop. The coil-loop topology now exists in the BOM (contactor LC1D09BD, Rs 1,226 incl. GST).

## 7. Grounding and EMC notes

- One 0 V reference on the Lane Controller; field inputs are optically isolated, so field-side 24 V return does not share the logic ground path through GPIO.
- PE: mains PE bonds the metal enclosure, DIN rail and SPD; DC 0 V is **not** bonded to PE except at one point at the PSU output (to be chosen at assembly; do not bond at both the LRS-200 and the LRS-100 outputs).
- Motor power and return run from the supply to the IBT-2 module terminals, off the Lane Controller PCB (the old J2/J3 are removed); join the logic ground at a single star point at the 24 V input terminal. Layout: power zone left, logic centre, connectors on the edge, antenna keep-out above the ESP32 module.
- RS485: shield grounded at one end only; stub-free; termination only at the PCB end and at the reader end if the cable is longer than a few metres.
- Motor: 100 nF at the motor terminals and ferrite on the motor cable are recommended additions, not on the current schematic (to be decided at bench: T-12).
- PoE and Ethernet shields terminate at the RJ45, not at the Lane Controller.
- Pi 5 and AI HAT+ switch noise is kept off the 24 V bus by the XL4015's own input capacitors; this is not simulated.
