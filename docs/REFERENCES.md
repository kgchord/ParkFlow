## Not verified (excluded from claims)
- EN 12453 numeric force limits (paywalled): not quoted.
- A public NPCI/IHMCL FASTag *technical* tag/reader specification: not found; only IHMCL's conformance statement (S13) is cited.
- IS 732:2019 on the BIS portal: edition taken from secondary sources (S8).
- Basic wind speed for Sonipat/Haryana: Delhi value (47 m/s) used, to be confirmed against IS 875-3 Fig. 1.
- Vendor listing claims (IP ratings, RS485, PoE, 850 nm IR, self-locking) for the RFID reader, camera, P10 module and wiper motor: see the Notes column of `bom/ParkFlow_BOM.xlsx`; several are not stated on the listings.
- Stage 1 competitor funding figures (Park+ "over 50 million US dollars"; Get My Parking pilots in Jaipur and Navi Mumbai): not re-verified. Public reports found on 25 Oct 2026 show separate Park+ rounds of about 11, 17 and 25 million US dollars and a 140 crore rupee Series C, but a total was not checked. These figures are not repeated in the Stage 2 documents.
- Park+ and other competitor claims in `PROBLEM_AND_JOURNEY.md` come from Stage 1 only.
